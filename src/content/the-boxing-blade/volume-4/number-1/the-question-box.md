# The Question Box

Q. How many times has Jack Dempsey been knocked out and by whom?—J. S., East Haddam, Conn.

A. Dempsey has been knocked out only once, that time by Jim Flynn in one round in 1917.

* * *

Q. Tell who have been knocked out by Jack Dempsey from 1915 to 1922, and number of rounds. Also who have lost decisions to him in that length of time.—J. S., East Haddam, Conn.

A. In 1915 and 1916 Dempsey knocked out Kid Hancock, one; Billy Murphy, one; Chief Gordon, six; Johnny Person, seven; Anamas Campbell, three; Joe Lions, nine; Fred Woods, four; George Copelin, seven; Andy Malloy, three; Two Round Gillian, one; Jack Downey, two; Boston Bear Cat, one; Battling Johnson, one; George Christian, one; Jack Koehn, four; Joe Bonds, ten; Dan Ketchell, five; Bob York, four.

In 1917 he knocked out Al Norton, one, and Charley Miller, one.

In 1918 he knocked out Homer Smith, one; Jim Flynn, one; Bill Brennan, six; Bull Sadee, one; Tom Riley, one; Dan Ketcher, two; Arthur Pelky, one; Kid McCarthy, one; Bob Devere, one; Porky Flynn, one; Fred Fulton, one; Terry Kellar, five; Jack Moran, one; Battling Levinsky, three; Porky Flynn, one; Carl Morris, one; Gunboat Smith, two.

In 1919 he knocked out Big Jack Hickey, one; Kid Harris, one; Kid Henry, one; Eddie Smith, one; Tony Drake, one; Jess Willard, three.

In 1920 he knocked out Terry Kellar, three; Billy Miske, three; Bill Brennan, twelve.

In 1920 he knocked out Georges Carpentier in four rounds.

In 1915 and 1916 Dempsey won decisions over Johnny Sudenberg, ten; Terry Kellar, ten; Andre Anderson, ten; Wild Burt Kenney, ten; John Lester Johnson, ten.

In 1917 he won decisions over Willie Meehan, four; Bob McAllister, four; Gunboat Smith, four; Carl Morris, four.

In 1918 he won on a foul from Carl Morris in the sixth round.

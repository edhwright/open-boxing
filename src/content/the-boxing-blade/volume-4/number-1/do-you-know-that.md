# Do You Know That——

Jack Curley had a stroke of heart failure when he heard that his wrestling friend, Muldoon, was about to be knocked off the throne of athletic supervision in New York.

* * *

Jack was the fellow who made wrestling popular in New York.

* * *

*Muldoon was the fellow who with his flying falls and folderols, together with the barring of several famous holds, put wrestling on the blink.*

* * *

Only last year Curley advised Muldoon that he (Curley) would be the leading factor of the wrestling world when he (Muldoon) would be sent back to his health farm at White Plains for repairs.

* * *

And it has come to pass, that just when we had Bill's wrestling career completed for publication along comes his going out party.

* * *

Now it isn't worth reading.

* * *

*No one said anything about his dealing. It was his shuffling we didn't like.*

* * *

Tom Gibbons has boxed Bill Miske four times already with results about the same.

* * *

At St. Paul they are talking of doing it again.

* * *

*Let us all now rise and sing "the farmer hauled another load away."*

* * *

There is to be a change on the boxing commission at Omaha.

* * *

Omaha has a population of about 200,000 and it is said that only one will be sorry of the change on the comish.

* * *

Meaning that he lost out by about 199,999.

* * *

*To make a chicken lay right cut its head off with the axe.*

* * *

When the New York Commission ruled a limit on prices charged at boxing shows, they did a great favor to the professional scalpers.

* * *

Boxing fans paid as much as $25.00 for $3.00 seats at the Villa-Goldstein Show.

* * *

Fine for the scalpers.

* * *

*"I'll see you later," said the judge, as he continued the bootlegger's trial.*

* * *

Farmer Lodge knocked two western heavyweights for several rows of ash cans last week.

* * *

Lodge has evidently quit farming and gone to fighting.

* * *

Dan Morgan has been accused of losing a ton of money on fast horses.

* * *

It's all a mistake, it's the slow ones that raised havoc with Danial.

* * *

Johnny Creeley turned out to be a good matchmaker for Charley Harvey.

* * *

*A crow is a brave bird,—it never shows a white feather.*

* * *

Bat Krause, the North Dakota K. O. king, did not win the 140-pound title but says he is willing to fight the winner.

* * *

*"I am cutting some figure in this world," said the chorus girl as she sat on the broken bottle.*

* * *

Abe Attell Goldstein positively refused to hit Pancho Villa.

* * *

Abe appeared like he didn't want to make Pancho sore.

* * *

* * *

Jersey Jones says that Ray Pryel has everything that a champion needs and more things than most champs have.

* * *

Jersey is a great booster for Ray. He further says that Pryel looks like a champion and fights like one.

* * *

Jersey is Pryel's manager.

* * *

*"Prohibition or no prohibition, here is where I take a drop," remarked the steeple jack as the rope broke.*

* * *

Billy McCarney has been reinstated by the New York Comish.

* * *

Billy was barred because he ballyhooed Tut Jackson too hilariously, previously to Tut's famous bout with Harry Wills at Brooklyn.

* * *

Bill Muldoon, in lifting McCarney's suspension, warned Billy not to get so enthusiastic over nothing next time.

* * *

*McCarney told Muldoon a few good stories and all was like new.*

* * *

We understand that Muldoon is oh, so much easier to get along with since election.

* * *

But what a tough bird Mr. Bill would be if Mr. Miller had been re-elected Governor of New York.

* * *

*"I'll open her for fifty," said the surgeon to the gambler, whose wife was afflicted with appendicitis.*

* * *

One good term deserves another. Chairman Eugene Buckley has been re-appointed for another three-year term as head of the Massachusetts Boxing Commission.

* * *

Martie Killilea is managing a heavyweight.

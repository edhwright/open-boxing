# An Earnest Wish

*Speed forward! Speed forward!<br>Oh time in thy flight,<br>And bring us a boxer<br>Jack Dempsey can fight.*

*Old timers, come back<br>From that echoless shore,<br>Give us some finish fights<br>Like we saw years before.*

*Give us genuine scraps,<br>Not mere exhibitions,<br>We want genuine champs,<br>Not those made by commissions.*

*Bring back the time<br>Where, upon our free (?) soil<br>We can view twenty-rounders<br>And the old battles royal.*

*Give us! Oh, give us!<br>A real he-man's fight<br>Without boxing commissions,<br>And seats out of sight.*

# Breezy Broadway Boxing Bits

Many boxers will get their Christmas box on the chin on Dec. 25th.

* * *

But such a Christmas box will not mean good cheer and turkey galore.

* * *

Except the victim who gets the K. O. is guaranteed a very fat loser's end.

* * *

You know some flopper don't give a rap as long as they corral the coin.

* * *

In fact, there seems to be an epidemic of tank divers nowadays.

* * *

What a fine collection of them flopped at Jimmy Johnston's Beef Trust Show.

* * *

But these ponderous truck drivers and deckhands drew a $6,100 gate all the same.

* * *

The Garden management got half the receipts for the burlesque show.

* * *

Rough House Curley McGuire played the leading clown in the final bout.

* * *

But how come the Commish to allow the Garden to run off two boxing shows in one week.

* * *

But the Garden always has more privileges than any other club in this state.

* * *

Wonder why? Probably the powers that be can explain why?

* * *

A lot of dubs burlesque boxing there week after week while the real stars are shut out.

* * *

Why all this favoritism and injustice?

* * *

One bout in the Garden is often worth three in the domineered smaller clubs, financially.

* * *

It's to be hoped under a new Commish that everybody will get a fair deal.

* * *

Some referees and judges work three and four times a week.

* * *

While others without a political pull do not get an assignment once a year.

* * *

New York managers who are not in the clique have to depend upon out-of-town clubs for matches.

* * *

While some outside impresarios in the clique get the cream here.

* * *

Jack Dempsey would like "To take" Harry Wills for his Christmas box.

* * *

But they are saving Jack and Harry for some fistic fireworks next July 4th.

* * *

That's the reason Paddy Mullins is turning down all offers for Wills to combat anybody but Dempsey.

* * *

He boxes most every day in a gym and does a lot of road work.

* * *

Which is rather premature, however, for a mill so far away.

* * *

Leo P. Flynn and Dave Shade have purchased adjoining houses on the Grand Concourse.

* * *

"The fly boys have gypped me out of 1,800 grand since I hit Broadway," says Flynn.

* * *

"And I want to be sure of a home for the missus and myself before I get another trimming," continued Leo.

* * *

They must have chloroformed the wise Leo to take him for 1,800 grand.

* * *

But frequently the sharpest managers fall like an ordinary sucker.

* * *

One of the greatest boxing promoters is said to be an easy mark for Wall Street sharks.

* * *

I could a tale unfold about several other wise birds who have lost thousands playing the stock market.

* * *

Sharp, clever men who have corralled big bankrolls in fistic ventures.

* * *

The height of fame for some fighters is to have their photos printed on the front page of the Boxing Blade.

* * *

But only those who are worthy of the honor obtain that distinction.

* * *

Izzy Faber, Fred Costain and Pete the Goat have refused to become boxing commissioners.

* * *

The punk managers don't allow boxing to interfere with their bootlegging ventures.

* * *

But between raving over fighters and hooch they have constant brainstorms.

* * *

It's a wonder the Commish doesn't land some of these nuts in the bughouse.

* * *

Moving pictures of the wild scenes in the offices of these ducks would amaze the outside world.

* * *

But listening to the flabbergasting chatter in one of the offices is enough to set a normal individual insane.

* * *

There's where you hear the wildest kind of million dollar schemes and the rankest roastology.

* * *

If Mr. Muldoon and Co. could only listen in at some of these roastology sessions he would resign his job at once.

* * *

Or maybe the autocratic chairman would order the whole flock to be shot at sunrise.

* * *

Wonder why Muldoon's secret service sleuths don't swoop down upon the hot roasters and pinch them all?

* * *

Jimmy Mash, the able manager of the Broadway Club, Brooklyn, is sore on some of the trainers.

* * *

The cuks who discourage and frighten boxers who are matched up at his club.

* * *

And with a lot of other discouraging bleating they finally persuade the timid boys to run out of a match.

* * *

That alleged $1,000,000 offer to Dempsey for three fights by Al Woods is evidently only a publicity joke.

* * *

Nobody takes it seriously, not even Al Woods himself.

* * *

The sharp shooters are still betting the suckers to a standstill in and out of the Garden.

* * *

One might as well try to stop the sun from rising as to prevent gambling in any kind of sport

* * *

"A Foolish Judge passeth a hasty sentence." So does a foolish Commish.

* * *

He that loseth a poor fight and a punk manager may still live to become great, if he's the goods.

* * *

An autocratic ruler of boxing today and tomorrow a——.

* * *

Jack Rogers is going to get Jack Britton to make a champion out of Tommy McAleer.

* * *

Al Lippe was unjustly suspended for defending himself in a cowardly assault at Syracuse some moons ago.

* * *

It's only right and proper for the Commish to re instate the veteran manager.

* * *

What would become of the various commissions if all the boxers went on a strike?

* * *

Or if the boxers had a powerful union and suspended the commissions?

* * *

New York managers are boycotting certain cities where they hold up the fighters' money.

* * *

Quite a few of our impresarios have been cheated by some of the flim flam out-of-town clubs.

# The Question Box

Q. Was Benny Leonard ever knocked out. If so, who by?—J. S., East Haddam, Conn.

A. Joe Shugrue knocked Leonard out in four rounds in 1912 and Frankie Fleming knocked him out in five rounds in 1913.

* * *

Q. Did Jess Willard ever fight Joe Cox and did Bill Brennan ever fight Harry Greb?—J. R., Sacramento, Calif.

A. Willard and Cox were matched in 1911 and Willard was stopped in the fifth round. Brennan and Greb met four times in 1919. Greb won the decision in a fifteen-round bout and the other three were ten round, no decision fights.

* * *

Q. What is the address of Leo P. Flynn?—D. H. A. B., Philadelphia.

A. Leo P. Flynn, 328 East Fourteenth street, New York City.

* * *

Q. Have Lew Tendler and Johnny Dundee ever fought? If so, name date and winner.—J. J. M., U. S. S. "New Mexico."

A. Tendler and Dundee fought a six-round, no decision bout in 1917.

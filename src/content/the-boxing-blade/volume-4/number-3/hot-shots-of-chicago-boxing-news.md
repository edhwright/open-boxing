# Hot Shots of Chicago Boxing News

Joe Lynch, bantam champion, is now in our midst and is flooded with offers. His arrival has caused a lot of interest among the bantams. Manager Eddie Meade closed matches with Joe Sanger in Milwaukee on Dec. 7 and Eddie Anderson at Davenport on Dec. 15. He will then hike back to Broadway and put his title at stake on Dec. 22 with Midget Smith in the Garden.

* * *

Rocky Joyce, a former New York featherweight youngster, has made rapid strides since his invasion here. So far he has won 16 out of 19 conflicts, always boxing twice a week or more. Joyce formerly boxed under the name of Munger.

* * *

Foxy Pal Moore used his head the other night to earn a draw with Harold Smith. It went around that Moore wasn't in any condition and instead of fighting his usual battle why Harold's advisers sent him in to fight Moore off his feet. All the while Moore kept piling up enough points to hold his own. No credit can be taken away from either one for on the showing each made, both now can step with the best of them. Moore showed his old time form and good headwork. It was his first battle since the Joe Lynch affair in Michigan City on Labor Day.

* * *

Weekly visitors were Jack Dempsey and Jack Kearns, Joe Lynch, Manager Eddie Meade and Trainer Connie McCarty, Vincent Porkoni and Manager Ray Campbell from Cleveland, Frankie Henke and Jimmy Mussey from Milwaukee, Dave Shade and Bob Smith on their way to Omaha.

* * *

Benny Berris, "The Battling Blonde Boy," 126-pounder, since his arrival from the East, has won nine contests. Berris was schooled carefully and now is ripe for the topnotchers. He is booked to battle K. O. Mars in Cincinnati on Dec. 18.

* * *

The fans are still talking about the sensational Eddie Anderson-Jack Eile battle. Promoter Jim Mullen will get a club with a larger seating capacity to handle the crowd of fans that are clamoring to witness this return fuss, Dec. 19. In the mean time Anderson will battle Champion Joe Lynch and Eile will take on Rocky Joyce.

* * *

Bob Smith, Leo P. Flynn's traveling representative, arrived with Dave Shade, who is to box Frankie Schoell in Omaha. Dave stated that he will do 140 pounds for Charley White if he is so anxious for a match. But he seems to evade the question about a Sailor Friedman tussle for which Promoter John Wagner is dickering.

* * *

Patsy Flanigan, the crack St. Louis bantam, is back from New York where he won a bout and hurt his hand, but after a two week layoff he is ready to tackle the best that Manager Joe O'Keefe can get for him. Anderson, Burman, Dundee and Mandell are the soft ones that Patsy wants to mingle with.

* * *

Jack Dempsey and "Jack" Kearns were in town last week for a few days and each donated to a local newspaper Christmas fund for the kiddies. While here they received that big offer from Al Woods, the theatrical man, and through it they gained a lot of publicity, but so far nothing has developed.

* * *

Joe Burman will get down to real training this time as he has a tough tussle with Tommy Murray who Eddie Stanton is importing from Philadelphia again. Murray gave Eddie Anderson a great battle and made a lot of friends. They will battle on the 15th of December. Burman's cigar store business has taken up a lot of his time.

* * *

Local boxers are getting a good play from the outside promoters as the number of out-of-town engagements that Sailor Friedman, Eddie Anderson, Bill Tate, Jack Eile, Bud Taylor, Sammy Mandell, Pal Moore, Harold Smith, and lot of other lessor lights are receiving from all parts of the country shows.

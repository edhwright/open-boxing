# The Question Box

Q. How many times has Johnny Kilbane been knocked out and why by?—J. S., East Haddam, Conn.

A. Kilbane has been knocked out once, by Benny Leonard in three rounds in 1917.

* * *

Q. In what round was the Nelson-Wolgast fight stopped?—G. O. S., Livingston, Mont.

A. Fortieth round.

* * *

Q. Did Bill Brennan whip Jack Dempsey?—O. Y., Sioux Falls, S. D.

A. No.

* * *

Q. Was Bud Logan ever knocked out?—G. K., Chicago.

A. No.

* * *

Q. How many times has Jack Dempsey been knocked out and by whom? How many times has Georges Carpentier been knocked out and by whom?—T. J., Montreal, Quebec.

A. Dempsey was knocked out by Jim Flynn in one round in 1917. Carpentier has been knocked out three times, by Gloria in 1909, by Dempsey in 1921 and by Battling Siki this fall. Gloria won in the sixth round, Dempsey in the fourth and Siki in the sixth.

* * *

Q. Did Harry Wills ever defeat Jack Thompson? If so, give date.—D. M., Boston.

A. Wills and Thompson have met five times. One was a ten round, no decision bout in 1916. Another was a six round, no decision bout in 1918. Wills won the decision in fifteen rounds in 1920 and another bout that year was called no contest. It went three rounds. Thompson claimed a foul in the fifth round at Denver in 1921 and the referee stopped the bout, declaring it no contest.

* * *

Q. Please publish the addresses of Jimmy Dunn and Biddy Bishop, managers.—W. McG., Terre Haute, Ind.

A. The address of Biddy Bishop is Box 540, Cincinnati, Ohio. His street address is 330 East Fourth street. Jimmy Dunn, 2433 West Twenty-fifth street, Cleveland, Ohio.

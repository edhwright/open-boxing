# When Jack Meets Ed

*Note—According to recent newspaper reports, Jack Dempsey has accepted an offer from Al H. Woods, theatrical producer, for three matches for a guarantee of approximately a million dollars for the champion. Two of these matches are to be with the best heavyweight fighters who are regarded as contenders that can be obtained, and the other is a mixed bout with Ed "Strangler" Lewis, champion catch-as-catch-can wrestler of the world. In the latter bout, Dempsey would be permitted to fight or wrestle, while Lewis would be confined to grappling. The object of the bout would be to decide which is the best means of self-defense, boxing or wrestling. Both have many supporters. Following is one writer's idea of such a match set forth in verse:*

While admirers sing the praises of their own exalted champs,<br>
Some argue that the "uppercut" will win the day from "clamps"<br>
Could Demon Dempsey rock the Strangler to Morpheus with a clout?<br>
Or could Lewis screw the headlock on till Dempsey's eyes pop out?

Picture in your minds eye two super-men of sport,<br>
Engaging in a mixed bout and to all tricks resort;<br>
With nothing barred in wallops, nothing barred in holds<br>
And pile driving punches against a python's folds.

If Dempsey misses his first punch and Lewis gets behind him<br>
And slips a wrist or hammerlock upon Jack to remind him<br>
That in the clinches Strangler always does his stuff<br>
And with a tweak, a gentle twist he treats the Demon rough.

But should the Dempsey wallop land and such is in the question,<br>
The resin from the canvas may cause Strangler indigestion;<br>
If the "heel" he gave at Wichita was ether to Zbyszko,<br>
A spank from the Demon's dark one will land him out by Frisco.

While struggling with might and main, and working in the clinches,<br>
The rabbit punch may stretch the Strangler's neck out several inches;<br>
A flying mare give Dempsey a glimpse of fleeting faces<br>
And cause him to recline and gaze out where a world of space is.

Should the grappler grip the tootsie and turn the trilby tweaking,<br>
The perspiration might burst forth and cause the pug some shrieking;<br>
But should the leaping Lewis meet a counter swift and certain,<br>
Bring forth the crepe, apply the ice, ring down the final curtain.

Against a champ who doesn't carry guns of such large bore,<br>
The matman would very likely put him down forevermore;<br>
But the vulnerable spots where Jack's blows are sure to land,<br>
Are all the same on head or hoof for each will wreck a man.

So if the snappy, sinful Socker meets the Adam's apple locker,<br>
Let's have it while our appetite is whetted up and keen;<br>
Then bring on the next new thriller, radio or plain fool killer—<br>
Say a bout between an aeroplane and the slippery submarine.
